# Third-party model assets

The Articulator Lab 3D reference includes geometry generated from VocalTractLab
and its JD3 speaker data, copyright Peter Birkholz and contributors, under
GPL-3.0-or-later. These assets are not covered by the repository's MIT license.
The collection includes six unchanged preset meshes and four explicitly labeled
derived teaching poses generated from JD3 parameter recipes. The derived poses
are modifications for illustration, not exact or validated English presets.

The pinned source, original notices, full GPL text, speaker input, and exporter
are provided with the assets. See [model provenance](third_party/vocaltractlab/README.md)
and [the export workflow](scripts/vtl/regenerate.sh). In the app, use **Model
credits & license** to view attribution and license terms.

The repository's MIT license continues to apply to its original code. This
notice does not grant an exception to the GPL terms for distributing a combined
application.
